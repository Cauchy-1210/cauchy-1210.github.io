I am an MSc student in Communications Engineering at **Nanyang Technological University (NTU)**, Singapore, advised by [Prof. Chau Yuen](https://blogs.ntu.edu.sg/chau-yuen/), and expect to graduate in January 2027. I carry out my research in the **COSMO Lab** under the supervision of Dr. Yufei Zhao.

My work is on **radar imaging and RF sensing**. The question I am currently working on is how a compact radar with only a *single receive channel* can still separate two targets that share the same range cell — a setting in which conventional processing has no angular information left to exploit. Rather than adding receive hardware, the approach codes the **transmit** side: four plane-spiral OAM radiators illuminate the scene in sequence, each carrying a distinct azimuthal phase slope, and the resulting equivalent-order diversity supplies the angular discrimination that a receive array would normally provide. I derived the factorized point-target response and the matching backprojection processor, and validated the method in simulation and on a stepped-frequency X-band measurement testbed. This work is described in [C1](/publications/).

Earlier, my undergraduate thesis at the **University of Sussex** (advised by Prof. Zhengguo Sheng) built an end-to-end RFID and IoT traceability system for textile production, replacing a manual paper-based process with closed-loop tracking from production through quality inspection. The system has since attracted commercial interest and is in discussion with an industry partner.
